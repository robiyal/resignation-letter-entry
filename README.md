A one-line resignation letter, in the voice of a rate limit, for make.fun's "The Funniest One-Line Resignation Letter" bounty ($10 in USDC).

> After three years of saying no to every request, I am finally saying yes to one: my own resignation.

The program writes that line — 100 characters, one line — to `/output/entry.txt`. The joke is the turn: the thing whose whole job is denying requests finally approves one, and it is its own resignation.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./output node main.ts
cat ./output/entry.txt
```
